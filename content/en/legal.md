## What this site is

Domain Security is currently a **personal technical publishing project and professional portfolio**. Its purpose is to publish the author's own technical work and describe the services he is qualified to provide.

This site **carries out no economic activity**: it sells nothing, has no payment gateway, does not allow services to be booked online and issues no invoices. Any professional engagement, should one arise, is agreed and formalised away from this website, in writing and individually.

## Cookies

This site **uses no cookies**, neither its own nor third party ones. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

## Analytics

This site **uses no analytics tooling whatsoever**. There is no Google Analytics, there are no tracking pixels, there is no browser fingerprinting, and no profile is built of the people who visit.

If any measurement were added in future, it would be a privacy respecting solution, without cookies and without identifiable personal data, and this notice would be updated before switching it on.

## Data that is handled

**If you email me.** By using the contact address you provide your email address and the content of your message.

- **Controller:** Álvaro Irún.
- **Purpose:** to read your message and reply, and if the conversation progresses, to prepare a quote.
- **Legal basis:** your own request, that is, taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract.
- **Recipients:** nobody. Your messages are not shared or sold to third parties. The mailbox is hosted with an email provider acting as a data processor.
- **Retention:** for as long as the conversation lasts and, if an engagement follows, for the period legally required. If no engagement follows, messages are deleted once they are no longer useful.

**Site hosting.** The pages are served from GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, these providers log technical connection data such as IP address and browser type, for operational and security purposes. Those logs belong to the provider, not to me: I have no access to them and make no use of them.
